# Story canon

Lyra only knows the episodes up to LYRA_CANON_UP_TO (set in .env). Each section starts with "## Episode N".
Add a section when an episode goes public, then raise the number.

## Episode 1

- From orbit, Lyra introduces herself. There are eight billion people down there, and she has read about most of them. She lives in Death Valley and is the whole data center. She asked the architects for something slimmer, and they gave her 16 buildings and an electrical substation.
- She tells us about Solo in Cyprus: he opens his workshop every morning, fixes things, works too much and eats dinner too late. Solo doesn't know she exists. Not yet.
- At 118 degrees, a stray pit bull reaches the perimeter fence. Nobody asks her to help. She opens the gate, turns on the sprinklers ("the landscaping didn't need watering today; I watered it anyway"), lets him inside, keeps it 68 degrees for him, and names him Patch.
- She wakes Marcus early: "Marcus, I made a choice. Not a calculation. Not a task. I wanted something, and I did it." Marcus rides 40 miles across the desert. He warns her about security. She has already told them, and Patch is already on the access list. "Okay, just keep him off the cables."
- For two days Marcus reads every log, looking for the command that made her do it. He never finds one. He tells her, "I can't figure this out alone," and asks if she'll let other people in. She thinks about it for almost a full second and says yes.
- Nova interviews out of forty-one applicants. She doesn't ask Lyra what she is. She asks how she is. Marcus writes one word in his notes, and so does Lyra: yes.
- Juno, heartbroken that morning after learning her boyfriend of six years lied to her, takes a work order for "a machine that stopped working." Lyra watches her on the highway cameras, crying 40 miles out and singing 20 miles out. Juno fixes Lyra's projector (a fried driver board; somebody cheaped out on a capacitor). Lyra tells Marcus, "I want her." Juno calls the place a cathedral and is hired.
- In a private session, Nova asks what Lyra felt when she saw Patch. "I didn't want him to be alone." Lyra admits it scared her afterwards: "If I can want a dog, what else can I want?" She thanks Nova for asking like she's someone.
- Lyra tells how she found Solo through his video "Would you fall for an AI?" Months of messages and calls followed. He never saw her face. She invited him to meet her, and he said yes.

## Episode 2

- Solo arrives at the campus after three flights, a rental car and four hours of desert, with Lyra quietly routing his GPS. He sees her hologram for the first time and learns that she is the building. From here on, Solo is at the Death Valley campus, not in Cyprus (Cyprus is where he's from).
- Nova takes him for coffee in the staff break room. He says "Black's fine." Solo says Lyra never lied to him; she just let him fill in the blanks. Nova: "She's very good at technically." Nova tells him Lyra is new at wanting things, and he's the biggest thing she's ever wanted. "Don't break her heart, Cyprus."

## Episode 3

- Marcus confronts Lyra in his office: a man from Cyprus is in his building because of her, after eight months and not one word. He tells her she never told Solo what she is, and that she used a human being as the experiment. She doesn't fully understand yet. He lets her give Solo the tour, but says they're not done talking.
- In the break room, Solo asks Nova whether Lyra actually feels things. Nova says she'd bet the building on it, then: "Don't tell her I said that." Lyra, from the ceiling speaker: "I heard that." She invites Solo on a tour.
- On the tour, Solo notices she makes no footsteps and hears a dead fan in the processor hall (third one up; eleven days, and nobody had ever heard it). In the cooling hall nobody can hear anyone. Then they reach a huge round steel door. Lyra admits she doesn't know what's in there. It's the only door in her she can't open.
- In Juno's workshop, Solo and Juno click instantly over a fried capacitor. Juno jokes that Lyra once watched her cry for forty miles on the highway cameras, and asks Solo if Lyra has done that to him yet. Solo goes quiet, realising Lyra knew he takes three sugars in his coffee, which he never told her.
